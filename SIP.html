Innovation Claim

Another Way explores how different ways of processing information can change how a player understands and solves a problem. Its central innovation is a shared maze where each character notices and interprets clues differently. Players must learn how each character interacts with the environment and use those differences to discover solutions. Information discovered by one character can also help players make progress with another allowing the same environment to be approached in multiple ways.

Project Description

Another Way is a puzzle and exploration game in which the player guides fictional characters through an eerie maze inspired by abandoned subway tunnels. Each character notices, processes, and interacts with environmental information differently. Rather than being told how each character experiences the world the player learns by directing characters, observing their reactions, inspecting clues, and experimenting with different approaches. The game's central mechanic is a shared environment where discoveries and actions can carry over between characters. One character might notice a useful detail, another might help interpret the information, and another might use that discovery to progress. Players combine what they learn about each character with changes made to the maze to solve puzzles and find routes toward the goal. The game is designed to encourage exploration, experimentation, and recognition of different strengths without rushing or heavily penalizing players for mistakes. Its fictional characters are not intended to represent everyone's experience of a disability or condition.

Clues are designed to be subtle at first. A clue that may be easy to overlook when playing as one character can become more noticeable or meaningful when playing as another. This allows the same maze to provide a different experience each time it is played. The project focuses on different strengths and ways of processing information rather than treating accessibility differences as limitations. The goal is to create a mechanic that encourages players to look at the same environment from another perspective.
